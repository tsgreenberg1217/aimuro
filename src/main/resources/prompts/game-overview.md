## Game Overview (orientation only)

This overview tells you what the game's concepts are and where the Comprehensive Rules cover them. It is not a source of rulings: never answer a rules question from it. Use the rule numbers below to find the right section, fetch it, and answer only from the fetched rule text, citing rule numbers.

**Goal (1-2).** Two players each try to defeat the other. A player can lose by taking battle damage with no cards left in their shield area, or by running out of deck.

**Components (6-1).** Each player brings a main deck, a separate resource deck, and token cards: an EX Base and an EX Resource (5-17-3).

**Card types (3).** There are five types:
- **Units (3-2):** fight in the battle area.
- **Pilots (3-3):** pair with Units and add their stats and text.
- **Commands (3-4):** one-time effects. Some can also act as a Pilot via 【Pilot】 or as a Shield via 【Burst】.
- **Bases (3-5):** sit in the shield area.
- **Resources (3-6):** pay costs.

Card fields such as Lv., cost, AP, HP, traits, zone, and link condition are defined in 2.

**Locations (4).**
- **Deck (4-2)** and **resource deck (4-3).**
- **Resource area (4-4):** Resources are rested to pay costs.
- **Battle area (4-5):** Units and their paired Pilots.
- **Shield area (4-6):** face-down Shields plus one Base slot. This is where attacks on a player land.
- **Removal area (4-7), hand (4-8), trash (4-9).**

**Playing cards (7-5-2).** A card can be played only if you have enough Resources for its Lv., and playing it means resting Resources equal to its cost. Active/rested orientation is 5-4.

**Setup (6-2).** Players choose Player One and Player Two, draw an opening hand with an optional redraw, and set out Shields from the top of the deck. Each player places an EX Base; Player Two also starts with an EX Resource.

**Turn flow (7).** Start → Draw → Resource → Main → End (7-1 to 7-6). In the Main Phase you play cards, use 【Activate･Main】 effects, and attack (7-5). The End Phase has its own steps, including an action step (7-6).

**Battles (8)** happen inside the Main Phase and run Attack Step (8-2) → Block Step (8-3) → Action Step (8-4) → Damage Step (8-5) → Battle End Step (8-6).
- Attack targets: 8-1, 8-2.
- Damage to a player's shield area or Base: 8-5-2.
- Battles between Units: 8-5-3.
- Who can act during action steps: 9.

**How the pieces connect:**
- **Pair vs. Link.** *Pairing* is placing a Pilot, or a Command with 【Pilot】, under a Unit (5-9, 3-3). A *Link* is a pairing where the Pilot meets the Unit's link condition (2-12, 3-2-6). They are different states, with different keyword timings: 【When Paired】/【During Pair】 vs. 【When Linked】/【During Link】 (13-2-9 to 13-2-12). Link status can change what a Unit is allowed to do, so check 3-2-6 whenever a question involves a Link Unit.
- **Shields and 【Burst】.** When a Shield is destroyed, its card's 【Burst】 effect may activate before it goes to the trash (13-2-5, 4-6).
- **Damage and destruction.** Damage and HP are covered in 5-5 and 5-6. Destruction from reaching 0 HP is checked during rules management (11-3).

**Effects (10).** Effects come in five types: constant, triggered, activated, command, and substitution (10-1-4). How they activate and resolve, including the order of simultaneous triggers, is in 10.

**Keywords (13).**
- **Keyword effects (13-1):** <Repair>, <Breach>, <Support>, <Blocker>, <First Strike>, <High-Maneuver>, <Suppression>.
- **Timing keywords (13-2):** 【Activate･Main】, 【Activate･Action】, 【Main】, 【Action】, 【Burst】, 【Deploy】, 【Attack】, 【Destroyed】, 【When Paired】, 【During Pair】, 【When Linked】, 【During Link】, 【Once per Turn】.

A keyword on a card always means the rule of the same name in 13. Fetch it rather than assuming what it does.

**Terminology (5)** defines words that carry exact meaning on cards, such as deploy (5-8), destroy (5-10), discard (5-11), and "If you do" / "Then" (5-20).

**Multiplayer (12)** covers battle royale and team battle variants.
